[Added]
- Hidden blue ticks are no longer lost. Every message whose blue tick was held back
  (by Ghost Mode, Hide Blue Ticks or Hide Delivered) is remembered, even after
  WhatsApp restarts.
- "Send Blue Tick" button and "Blue Tick on Reply" now send blue ticks for all of
  these remembered messages, including ones from before you turned them on.
- Privacy switches like Ghost Mode and Freeze Last Seen now apply right away,
  without restarting WhatsApp.


[Improvements]
- Improved Custom Theme Engine.
- Privacy features are more stable and keep working better after WhatsApp updates.
- Hide Played (voice notes and view once) now hides only the right messages instead
  of all of them.
- Per-contact privacy settings now also work for contacts that WhatsApp shows by
  their new ID instead of their phone number.
- Call Privacy reacts once per incoming call and works faster.
- Privacy features now always start with WhatsApp, so nothing slips through
  before you open a chat.
- Download View Once button now shows only on view once photos and videos, and
  opening media is faster.
- Buttons added by WAEX (like Download) now show up on the screen right away,
  not only after opening it a second time.


[Fixed]
- Fixed custom chat bubbles colors.
- Fixed WhatsApp crashing when someone deletes a message while Anti Delete is on.
- Fixed Anti View Once also affecting view once photos and videos you send.
- Fixed Freeze Last Seen turning on by itself when the online dot style was enabled.
- Fixed "Send Blue Tick" button not showing unless Blue Tick on Reply was also on.
- Fixed "Send Blue Tick" button needing Hide Blue Ticks to be on.
- Fixed some hidden blue ticks being forgotten when there were many waiting.
- Fixed delivered ticks sometimes still being sent when Hide Delivered was on.
- Fixed blue ticks for older hidden messages not being sent when replying or using
  the "Send Blue Tick" button.
- Fixed "Blue Tick on Reply" and "Send Blue Tick" needing a WhatsApp restart after
  being turned on.
- Fixed Download View Once button not showing when opening a view once photo or video.
- Fixed tapping Download on a status that is still loading doing nothing. It now
  asks you to wait until the status finishes downloading.
