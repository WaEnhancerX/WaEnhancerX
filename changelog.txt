[Added]
- Hidden blue ticks are no longer lost. Every message whose blue tick was held back
  (by Ghost Mode, Hide Blue Ticks or Hide Delivered) is remembered, even after
  WhatsApp restarts.
- "Send Blue Tick" button and "Blue Tick on Reply" now send blue ticks for all of
  these remembered messages, including ones from before you turned them on.
- Privacy switches like Ghost Mode and Freeze Last Seen now apply right away,
  without restarting WhatsApp.
- New Bubble Colors screen with a live chat preview and separate colors for sent and
  received messages.
- Custom themes support more WaEnhancer theme features: color replacement, tint
  states, position offsets, rgb()/rgba() colors and the DPI set in a theme.
- Spanish and Latin American Spanish translations.


[Fixes]
- Fixed WhatsApp crashing when someone deletes a message while Anti Delete is on.
- Fixed Anti View Once also affecting view once photos and videos you send.
- Fixed Freeze Last Seen turning on by itself when the online dot style was enabled.
- Fixed "Send Blue Tick" button not showing unless Blue Tick on Reply was also on,
  and needing Hide Blue Ticks to be on.
- Fixed blue ticks for older hidden messages not being sent when replying or using
  the "Send Blue Tick" button.
- Fixed delivered ticks sometimes still being sent when Hide Delivered was on.
- Fixed Download View Once button not showing when opening a view once photo or video.
- Fixed tapping Download on a status that is still loading doing nothing. It now
  asks you to wait until the status finishes downloading.
- Fixed Download saving a different status when the status changed right after tapping.
- Fixed custom chat bubble colors not being applied.
- Fixed custom themes not loading in Per Target App mode, and large theme images
  making the chat input bar too big.
- Fixed double tap reactions sometimes reacting to the wrong message.
- Fixed Ghost Mode, Freeze Last Seen and DND restarting WhatsApp before the setting
  was saved.
- Fixed per-contact call blocking not working in some cases.
- Fixed Delete for Me sometimes not keeping a copy of the message.
- Fixed online dots being placed wrongly on some chat rows.
- Fixed the profile photo of the wrong person being shown or saved in some cases.
- Fixed voice notes turning the screen off when Disable Proximity is on.
- Fixed custom emoji fonts breaking when a download was incomplete.
- Fixed Harmful App warning by Play Protect.


[Improvements]
- Privacy features are more stable and keep working better after WhatsApp updates.
- Privacy features now always start with WhatsApp, so nothing slips through
  before you open a chat.
- Hide Played (voice notes and view once) now hides only the right messages.
- Per-contact privacy settings now also work for contacts that WhatsApp shows by
  their new ID instead of their phone number.
- Call Privacy reacts once per incoming call and works faster.
- Download View Once now shows only on view once media, and opening media is faster.
- Buttons added by WAEX (like Download) now show up on the screen right away.
- Custom themes no longer slow down scrolling.
- Larger text sizes fit better in the bottom bar and dashboard.
- On-device transcription libraries are now downloaded only when needed.
- VirusTotal scan results are attached to every release.
